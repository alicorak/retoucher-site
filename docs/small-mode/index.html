How to use it

  1. Press the shrink button in the panel's header.
  2. The panel becomes a small bar; drag it by Figma's title bar.
  3. Click the bar to open the full panel again.

Good to know

  • The bar shows when a note needs your answer, or how many are in progress.
  • Retoucher opens the way you left it.
