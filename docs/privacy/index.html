Stays on your computer

The panel talks to a bridge on your own computer and nothing else. Notes, screenshots and files never leave your machine.

Figma panelin your Figma
Bridgelocalhost:4747
Your agenton your computer
Notes, screenshots and files stay in ~/.retoucher

How to use it

  1. The agent plugin starts the bridge on localhost:4747 when your agent opens.
  2. The first Figma panel that connects pairs with the bridge and gets a key.
  3. From then on the bridge only answers requests that carry that key.

Good to know

  • Notes, screenshots and attachments are kept in ~/.retoucher.
  • Websites you visit can reach localhost too, so the key matters: without it a page can't read or send notes, or answer permission prompts.
  • Need to pair another panel? Ask your agent to run retoucher unpair.
  • The Figma comments option uses your personal Figma token, stored on your computer only.
