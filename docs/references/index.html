How to use it

  1. Connect a references MCP to your agent (Mobbin, for example).
  2. Open Options → References and pick 4, 8 or 12.
  3. Send the note. The references land next to your selection and show in the reply as thumbnails.

Good to know

  • Thumbnails open the screen at its source.
  • Without a references MCP the agent says so in the reply and stops.
