Areas and pins

Point at a part of a screen instead of a layer: drag a box around it or drop a pin, like a pinned Figma comment.

How to use it

  1. Select a frame, or nothing for what is on screen.
  2. Press Mark area next to the selection in the panel. A big picture of the frame opens.
  3. Drag to mark an area, or click to drop a pin. Add as many as you need.
  4. Press Add, then write the note and send.

Good to know

  • The agent gets each area's crop, its position, the screen it sits in and the layer underneath.
  • Number your areas in the note (“1: less glare, 2: bolder”) when you mark more than one.
  • Backspace removes the last mark.
